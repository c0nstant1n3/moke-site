Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026

Moke: Learn Georgian ("Moke") does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No network requests: the app works entirely offline

Data on your device

Your learning progress (for example which letters and phrases you have practised) is saved only on your iPhone. It never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

TestFlight

If you test a beta through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and feedback you choose to submit with the developer, under Apple's privacy policy. These are used only to fix problems.

Children

Moke collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app changes.
