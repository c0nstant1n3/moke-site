Moke: Learn Georgian

A calm, free iPhone app for reading and speaking everyday Georgian.

  • The 33 letters of the Mkhedruli alphabet, one at a time
  • Around 100 everyday phrases with clear transliteration
  • Short practice and spaced review so it sticks
  • Works offline. No account, no ads, no tracking, no subscription

Moke is currently in beta on TestFlight. Feedback, corrections and ideas are very welcome: moke.feedback@proton.me
